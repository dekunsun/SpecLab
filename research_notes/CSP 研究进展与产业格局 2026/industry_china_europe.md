# China and Europe/UK industry landscape: AI crystal structure prediction (CSP) and materials discovery, 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-10-22. Labels used below: VERIFIED = primary filing, official release or institutional page; SELF-REPORT = company or partner claim with no independent data; MEDIA = secondary press or broker report. Scope: China, EU, UK. US players appear only where they connect to China or Europe (Periodic Labs/Kimi, Orbital).

## Q1. China: what each player actually does (CSP and materials), products, results, team origins

### Takeaway
XtalPi (晶泰, 2228.HK) is the only listed Chinese company in this space with audited revenue. Most of that revenue comes from pharma. Molecular CSP is now a small "value-added service" next to its larger robotic-lab ("Physical AI") and pipeline businesses, and its materials work so far consists of robotic-lab installs plus a JinkoSolar perovskite JV. DP Technology (深势) and AISI (北京科学智能研究院) are the main Chinese builders of atomistic foundation models (the DPA series) and scientific-computing platforms (Bohrium/玻尔). Big Chinese tech companies (Huawei, Tencent, Alibaba, ByteDance) have no visible crystal-structure products. The closest thing from a large lab is Shanghai AI Lab's Intern-S2-Preview, which can generate crystal structures.

## Q2. Europe/UK: what each player does, products, results, team origins

### Takeaway
CuspAI (Cambridge) is by far the best-capitalised European AI materials company: >$650M raised and a $2.6B valuation (Jul 2026). Its moat is distribution through the 45+ member Foundry and exclusive AI training rights to the CSD and ICSD structure databases, rather than published discovery results. Chemify (Glasgow) has the most concrete physical automation. Most other European startups (Entalpic, Dunia, Materials Nexus, Intellegens, Quantistry) are small, with seed or Series A funding of €3-12M. Avant-garde Materials Simulation remains the reference vendor for pharma molecular CSP.

## Q4. Closed-loop and self-driving labs with published throughput

### Takeaway

## Q6. Government programs and national labs
